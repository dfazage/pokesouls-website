A souls-like that lets you learn

Readable, punishing combat, Pokémon you actually care about, a hostile world. All playable in your browser.

Every death makes sense

Enemy attacks are announced by a circle on the ground. Dodge at the last second, parry, riposte. No cheap hits, no inflated HP faking difficulty.

Three Pokémon, one on the field

Switch whenever you want. Right after a perfect dodge, the incoming Pokémon fires a free entry attack, and elemental marks make enemies react: Grass then Fire gives Blaze, Fire then Water gives Steam.

A world that closes in on you

Six large maps, shortcuts that open behind you, a bonfire every ten to fifteen minutes. When a boss falls, its world changes.

Evolution is earned

No automatic evolution every level. It is rare, spectacular, and it changes how you play your Pokémon.

Six maps, six guardians

Each map closes in around a sanctuary. Each boss has its own universe, phases and traps.

Build your team of three

Four combat styles, no required composition. A Water Pokémon can hold the line, shoot from afar or heal.

  • Assault

    Fast melee, combos, backstabs.

  • Bulwark

    Guard, high poise, area control.

  • Ranged

    Shots, traps, area spells.

  • Support

    Heals on entry, cleansing, aura.
