A souls-like that lets you learn

Readable bosses, a duo you switch on the beat, and builds you shape yourself.

Boss fights worth dying for

Real-time duels against legendaries built to be read, not guessed. Hold your guard until the mark, break their posture, punish the opening. Every boss has a rhythm you learn a little more with each attempt.

Two Pokémon, one rhythm

Switch right after a perfect dodge or parry and your partner crashes in with a free entry attack. Stack types for reactions: Grass then Fire gives Blaze, Fire then Water gives Steam. Pair the same type for a resonance bonus. Fill the Link gauge and your resting partner steps in for a few seconds. Lose both and the run is over.

Rest, build, evolve

Bonfires heal both Pokémon, bring the enemies back and let you spend essence. Slot two TMs on each Pokémon, give it a held item, add vitamins. Evolution is never automatic: it is rare, it costs, and some Pokémon let you choose their form.

Worlds that feel alive

Day turns to night and weather rolls in. Enemy camps guard their loot, a wandering mini-boss patrols the map, and each world opens with its own arrival cinematic. Every map takes the look of the legendary who rules it, from frozen islands to an Edo-era bell tower.

One region per chapter

PokéSouls is told in five chapters, one for each region. Kanto is playable, Johto is being built now. The other three stay locked until their turn.

Two guardians, playable now

Each map closes in around a sanctuary. Step into the mist and the guardian wakes. Captured in the current build.

  • Articuno

    Foam Islands · Kanto

    Fight Articuno on its frozen nest. Read its wind-ups, dodge, and swap partners to keep the pressure on.

  • Raikou

    Bell Tower · Johto

    Face Raikou under the red maples of the Bell Tower, the first Johto guardian. Thunder, speed and a duel that punishes greed.

Build your duo

Three combat styles and no required composition. Support lives in your TMs, so any Pokémon can heal or shield.

  • Assault

    Fast melee, combos, backstabs.

  • Bulwark

    Guard, high poise, shield charge.

  • Ranged

    Shots, explosions, area spells.
