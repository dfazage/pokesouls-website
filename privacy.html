Privacy

What we collect

Your email address, the site language you picked and, if you choose them, your favourite three Pokémon. Nothing else: no tracking cookies, no ads, no analytics.

Why

Only to message you when PokéSouls launches, and to see which Pokémon people like most. Your email is never sold or shared.

Where and for how long

Data is stored with Supabase in a database that is closed to the public. It is deleted at the latest 12 months after the launch message is sent.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your email at any time by sending a direct message to @pokesouls.fun on Instagram. Include the address you want removed.

En français

On collecte ton email, la langue choisie et, si tu le fais, ton trio de Pokémon. Pas de cookie de suivi, pas de publicité, pas d’outil d’analyse.
